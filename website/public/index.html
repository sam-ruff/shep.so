Mail and calendar for your desktop

A little more room for your inbox.

Shep brings your mail accounts and calendars into one calm, fast workspace. Open source, comfortable with a mouse or a keyboard, and yours to arrange.

Get Shep Open the demo full screen

Free and open source · Linux, macOS and Windows · MIT licensed

The everyday, considered

Everything in its place.

The familiar things you need, with space to focus on what matters.

One inbox for every account

Bring IMAP and POP3 accounts together in a unified inbox, or browse each one on its own. Replies thread into conversations.

Instant, with Undo

Archive, move and flag without waiting on the server. Every change shows at once, syncs in the background and can be undone.

Search that finds it

Ranked search across every folder of your downloaded mail, forgiving of typos, with Find inside the message you are reading.

Your calendars alongside

Google Calendar and CalDAV in the same window, with a month view and a calm daily agenda.

A workspace that fits

Resize panes, drag mail into folders, remap shortcuts and choose Light, Dark or your own colours.

Private by default

Remote images stay blocked until you allow them. Encrypted backups go to a folder, Google Drive, S3, SFTP or FTP.

A little perspective

Your plans, alongside your mail.

See the month at a glance or settle into today's agenda. Events from Google Calendar and CalDAV sit in the same familiar workspace, in Light or Dark.

Make yourself at home

Get Shep.

Copy one command, paste it into a terminal and Shep appears in your applications menu.

Shep for Linux

Installs for your user, with a launcher in GNOME, KDE and other desktops. Paste the command into a terminal.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sam-ruff/shep.so/main/scripts/install-release-linux.sh | bash

Full installation guide ↗

Shep for macOS

Installs Shep.app into your Applications folder using the tools built into macOS. Paste the command into Terminal.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sam-ruff/shep.so/main/scripts/install-release-macos.sh | bash

Full installation guide ↗

Shep for Windows

Installs for your user with a Start menu shortcut. Paste the command into PowerShell.

& ([scriptblock]::Create((Invoke-RestMethod https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sam-ruff/shep.so/main/scripts/install-release-windows.ps1)))

Full installation guide ↗
