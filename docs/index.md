# Meet Shep

Your email and calendar, together in a calm native desktop app.

![Shep's unified inbox and message reader in light mode](images/mail-light.webp)

Read across multiple accounts, find messages quickly, and reply with attachments. Keep your calendar alongside your inbox. Choose your theme, resize the panes, and use the mouse or your own shortcuts.

## Get going

- [Install Shep](installation.md)
- [Read and send mail](mail.md)
- [Connect your calendar](calendar.md)
- [Set up backups](backups.md)

Shep is still in development. Check the [current limits](limits.md) before relying on it for your daily mail.
